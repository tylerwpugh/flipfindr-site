Sending from your own email

Why FlipFindr asks to connect your Gmail

An offer should arrive from the person making it. When you connect your Google Account, FlipFindr sends each approved offer from your own address, so the listing agent sees your name and can simply reply to you.

FlipFindr requests one permission, send email on your behalf, and nothing more:

  • It cannot read, search or open any message in your mailbox.
  • It sends only the offers you have reviewed and approved, one at a time.
  • You can disconnect it from your Google Account at any time.

Questions

Which MLS does it work with?

Any MLS you can export a CSV from. Columns are mapped when you import, so the field names your MLS uses do not need to match anyone else's.

Do I need my own purchase contract?

Yes, and that is deliberate. You upload the contract you already use in your state, and map its fields once. FlipFindr fills your paperwork, not a generic template.

Can it read my email?

No. FlipFindr asks for one Google permission, which allows sending only. It cannot read, search, open or delete anything in your mailbox, and it does not request a permission that would let it.

Does it send offers automatically?

No. You review each offer and the filled contract, and nothing goes out until you approve it.

Do I need a CRM?

No. If you use one, FlipFindr can log each sent offer against the right contact. If you do not, everything still works.

How long does setup take?

You set it up yourself, and it takes about twenty minutes: connect your email, upload your contract and map its fields once, then import your first list. Nothing needs a call with anyone.
