A few useful details

Before your first import.

What to bring, how the workflow fits, and where you stay in control.

Do I need access to the MLS?

You need an MLS spreadsheet export to bring into FlipFindr. The workflow starts with your exported listings; FlipFindr is not presented here as a replacement for MLS access.

What does the listing score tell me?

It helps prioritize listings using signals such as price cuts, days on market, and listing language. Review the details behind a listing before deciding what to offer. The score is not a property valuation or a prediction that an offer will be accepted.

Is “Your Number” the asking price?

No. The table keeps the asking price and the offer number you’re working with in separate columns. The gap shows how far those numbers are apart. Review your assumptions and terms before preparing an offer.

Do I use my own purchase contract?

Yes. Bring the contract you use in your market. FlipFindr maps property and offer details into your template, and you review the completed contract before sending it.

Does it send offers automatically?

No offer goes out without your review and approval. You choose the property, check the terms and recipient, and approve the message before it sends from your connected Gmail account.

Do I need a separate CRM?

No separate CRM is required to use FlipFindr’s offer history. Keep activity tied to each property, and use the context when following up with the listing agent.

What does the trial cost?

The first seven days are free. A card is required to start the trial, and the monthly plan is $199 after it ends. The first 10 members keep that founding price for as long as they stay subscribed; after that, the price is $299/month. You can cancel anytime.
