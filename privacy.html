FlipFindr

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

FlipFindr is software for real estate investors. It scores MLS listings for signs of a motivated seller, prepares a purchase offer and the accompanying contract, and sends that offer by email to the listing agent from the user's own email address.

This policy describes what data FlipFindr handles, where it is stored, and what it is used for. It describes the software as it actually behaves.

1. Google user data

FlipFindr requests one Gmail permission: gmail.send. That permission allows an application to send mail on your behalf. It does not allow it to read, search, list, download, modify or delete any message in your mailbox, and FlipFindr does none of those things.

It also requests openid and email, which let it see your Google account's email address. That address is used only to show you which Gmail account is connected.

Connecting your Gmail

Gmail is connected only when you press Connect Gmail in FlipFindr's settings and approve on Google's own screen. Google then gives FlipFindr a refresh token, which lets it send on your behalf until you disconnect. FlipFindr stores that token on its server, encrypted (AES-256), and tied to your FlipFindr account. It is never shown to you, your browser or anyone else, and it is never written to a log. The short-lived access token Google issues for each send is used once and not stored.

What we do with it

When you review an offer and choose to send it, FlipFindr uses gmail.send to deliver that one message from your address to the listing agent you selected, with the purchase contract attached. The message is composed in the application and shown to you before it goes. Nothing is sent without that action.

What we do not do with it

  • We do not read your mail. The permission does not permit it and the software does not attempt it.
  • We do not send anything you have not reviewed and approved.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning models.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade Google user data, and we do not transfer it to anyone except as needed to deliver the message you asked us to send.

What is retained

For your Google connection, FlipFindr keeps the encrypted refresh token, the connected email address, and the permissions Google granted. These are deleted when you disconnect.

For each offer it sends, FlipFindr keeps the identifier Gmail returns, together with the recipient address, the subject line, the property address, the MLS number and the offer amount, so that a sent offer can be traced afterwards and is not sent twice. This record is kept in your FlipFindr account. If you connect a CRM, it is also written there. The body of the message is not stored by FlipFindr. It is an ordinary email in your own Sent folder.

FlipFindr's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Services the software contacts

  • Google (Gmail API and Google sign-in) — to connect your Gmail and to send an offer you have approved.
  • Clerk — to sign you in to FlipFindr.
  • FlipFindr's server (hosted on DigitalOcean) — to store your account data and your encrypted Google connection.
  • Your CRM — if you connect one, to record the offer against the relevant contact.
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com — to load the PDF library used to fill contracts in your browser.
  • Google Maps — only when you click a map link for a property.

4. Retention and deletion

Your Google connection is deleted from our server the moment you press Disconnect in FlipFindr, and FlipFindr also revokes it at Google. Data in your FlipFindr account stays until you remove it in the application or ask us to delete your account at Tyler@flipfindr.io, which we do within 30 days. Data held in your browser stays until you clear it. Records written to your CRM are yours and are governed by your own CRM settings.

5. Revoking access

You can disconnect Gmail at any time with Disconnect in FlipFindr's settings, or at Google Account permissions. Either one stops FlipFindr from sending any further mail on your behalf immediately. It does not delete offers already sent, which are ordinary emails in your own Sent folder.

6. Security

All traffic between your browser, FlipFindr's server and Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Google refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with a key that exists only on FlipFindr's server, and each one is bound to its own account so that it cannot be used by any other. Every request to your account data requires you to be signed in, and an account can read only its own data.

7. Children

FlipFindr is a business tool and is not directed to anyone under 18.
